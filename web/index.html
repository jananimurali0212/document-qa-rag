  • Zero hallucination guarantee

Hit Rate@K

$$\text{Hit@K} = \frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_{q \in Q} \mathbb{I}(\text{Top-}K_q \cap G_q \neq \emptyset)$$

Measures whether at least one ground-truth passage appears within the top-K candidate list.

Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)

$$\text{MRR} = \frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_{q \in Q} \frac{1}{\text{rank}_q}$$

Evaluates where the first relevant ground-truth item lands in the retrieved candidate list.

Recall@K

$$\text{Recall@K} = \frac{|\text{Top-}K_q \cap G_q|}{|G_q|}$$

Quantifies the proportion of all relevant passages captured by the top-K candidates.

Precision@K

$$\text{Precision@K} = \frac{|\text{Top-}K_q \cap G_q|}{K}$$

Measures the purity of retrieved candidates, avoiding noisy distractors.
